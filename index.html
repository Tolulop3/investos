📖 HOW TO READ THE BRIEF TAB

The Brief tab is your morning command centre. It shows today's picks, ranked by conviction score (0–100). Higher score = more signals aligned.

Score tiers: 90–100 = High conviction (multiple signals). 75–89 = Strong. 55–74 = Watch. Below 55 = filtered out.

[FHSA] picks = conservative, low volatility, suitable for home savings timeline. [TFSA] picks = growth-oriented.

Deployment calculator — enter how much you have to invest today. The system splits it across buckets automatically.

📖 HOW TO USE LOOKUP

Type any ticker in the Lookup tab to get a full analysis on demand — not just today's screened picks.

TSX stocks: add .TO suffix → AEM.TO, ENB.TO, RY.TO

US stocks: no suffix → NVDA, AAPL, PLTR

Crypto: BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD

The TRADE card answers: should I buy this now for a short-term move? The INVEST card answers: is this worth holding long term?

📖 ACCOUNT SETUP — WEALTHSIMPLE

FHSA (First Home Savings Account): Tax-free savings for first home. $8,000/yr contribution room. Invest in conservative Floor picks only. Hard rule: never let a position drop more than 16% from purchase price.

TFSA (Tax-Free Savings Account): Tax-free growth on everything. No withdrawal restrictions. Use for Model picks, Swing trades, and income-generating Floor positions.

Stop losses: Set these the moment your buy fills. Not optional — they protect your FHSA from the 16% limit.

📖 READING YOUR PICKS

EQ: STRONG/SOLID/WEAK — Earnings Quality badge. Grades the company's financial reporting across 5 dimensions. STRONG adds points. WEAK subtracts.

🩳 SHORT % — Short interest. 10–19% = caution. 20%+ = high risk (but potential squeeze). Already penalised in score.

EARNS Xd — Earnings date warning. Orange ⚠️ means earnings within 14 days — binary event risk. Swing trades blocked within 7 days.

AGING/STALE/DEGRADING — Score decay. Signal was fresh days ago but hasn't improved. DEGRADING = 14+ days stale, −15pts penalty.

RS Rating — Relative Strength vs 177 stocks screened. RS 90 = outperforming 90% of universe. CANSLIM requires RS 80+.

📖 SIGNAL QUALITY FILTERS

Graham Intrinsic Value — For FLOOR picks, the system estimates what a stock is theoretically worth. If the price is more than 60% above intrinsic value, it gets hard-rejected from FHSA. 40–60% above = graduated penalty.

Factor Conflict Detector — Catches dangerous combinations: RS 90+ with EQ WEAK (speculative), high value score with declining revenue (value trap), high short interest with weak fundamentals.

Half-life Decay — Signals fade at different rates: news = 5 days, breakout = 10 days, earnings quality = 30 days, value = 90 days. Fresh signals (score rising) are exempt.

Volume Accumulation — Ratio of volume on up-days vs down-days over 20 sessions. High ratio = institutional buying quietly. Low ratio = distribution.
